About UniNest

More than just a housing platform.

We connect students with property owners and help them discover accommodation options near their campuses in Freetown.

Our purpose

Helping students feel at home

Finding a place to live while studying can be stressful. UniNest Freetown brings accommodation information into one accessible place, so students can compare options, understand rental details and contact property owners.

Our mission

Make student housing safer, simpler and more transparent.

Our vision

Help every student find a secure place to live and study.
